The SeedBuddy desktop app, for a Mac with Apple Silicon.

1. Download `SeedBuddy-…-mac-arm64.dmg` below, open it and drag SeedBuddy into Applications.
2. Open SeedBuddy. macOS says it cannot verify the app, which carries no Apple developer signature: choose Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway beside SeedBuddy. This is asked once.
3. Enter the address of your workbench and sign in.

See [Desktop app](https://github.com/i-richardwang/SeedBuddy/blob/main/docs/desktop.md) for what the app does.

SeedBuddy 桌面应用，适用于 Apple 芯片的 Mac。

1. 下载下方的 `SeedBuddy-…-mac-arm64.dmg`，打开后把 SeedBuddy 拖进「应用程序」。
2. 打开 SeedBuddy。应用没有 Apple 开发者签名，macOS 会提示无法验证：点「完成」，再到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」，点 SeedBuddy 旁的「仍要打开」。只需操作一次。
3. 输入工作台地址并登录。
